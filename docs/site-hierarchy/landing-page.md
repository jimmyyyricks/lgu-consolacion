# Landing Page

Route: `/` (`frontend/app/pages/index.vue`)

The homepage is the site entry point. Sections appear in this order:

1. **Welcome** — municipal introduction with links to [Services](services.md), [government actions](about-page.md), and [Transparency](transparency.md).
2. **Resident guides** — quick starting points for common information and services.
3. **Latest news** — featured news carousel; items open their detail route. “All news” opens [News](news-tourism.md).
4. **Services and links** — searchable links from the shared [service directory](services.md).
5. **Transparency and FOI** — links to the local transparency portal and the external Philippine FOI portal.
6. **Weather and reminders** — local visit reminders; weather is informational and not live.
7. **Tourism preview** — story links and the full [Tourism page](news-tourism.md).
8. **People's Corner** — routes for help, services, public information, and feedback.

Municipal background, programs, leadership, and officials are on [About Consolacion](about-page.md). The global [header and footer](header-navigation.md) provide routes into each major page group.
