# News and Tourism

## News

- `/news` — news and announcements archive.
- `/news/:slug` — story detail.

News appears in the homepage carousel and tourism preview links where applicable. Selecting an item opens its slug route; “All news” opens `/news`.

## Tourism

- `/tourism` — searchable visitor information and local stories.
- `/tourism/:slug` — story detail.

The homepage tourism preview links to story details and `/tourism`. Tourism pages also link back to news and government actions.

Both sections use shared public-content records. Their current local data source and CMS boundary are documented in [Business logic](../business-logic/public-content.md).
