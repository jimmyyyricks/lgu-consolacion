# Header and Navigation

The global header, utility bar, and footer are rendered by `app.vue`; navigation items are defined in `LguHeader.vue` and `LguSiteFooter.vue`.

## Header

| Item           | Destination                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Site identity  | `/`                                                                                                                                             |
| About          | `/about`; tabs cover the Citizen's Charter, Mayor's Office, Vice Mayor's Office, municipal officials, office directory, and government actions. |
| Transparency   | `/transparency`; menu links to every [public-record archive](transparency.md).                                                                  |
| Tourism        | `/tourism`                                                                                                                                      |
| Staff sign in  | `/login`; opens the [staff workspace flow](staff-cms.md).                                                                                       |

## Footer

The footer links to services, forms, government updates at `/about/actions`, [About](about-page.md), the Citizen's Charter section, municipal officials and their office directory, news, tourism, and public records. It also provides municipal contact details and staff sign-in.

On small screens the header menu is shown as a collapsible navigation; the destinations remain the same.
